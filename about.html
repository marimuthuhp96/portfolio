About Me

The Story Behind the Data

My journey into data analytics began with a curiosity about how data can explain patterns, solve problems, and support better decisions. After completing my Master of Science in Data Analytics, I focused on turning that curiosity into practical skills by working on real-world datasets and end-to-end analytics projects.

Throughout my learning journey, I have built interactive dashboards in Power BI and Excel, analyzed data using SQL and Python, and applied data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and machine learning techniques to uncover meaningful insights. I enjoy breaking down complex datasets into clear, actionable information that businesses can use with confidence.

Beyond traditional analytics, I enjoy exploring modern AI technologies. I have worked on projects involving Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and GraphRAG, combining data analytics with AI to create intelligent, insight-driven applications. These projects strengthened my ability to bridge data engineering, analytics, and AI into practical solutions.

Today, I am seeking opportunities as a Data Analyst, where I can continue learning, contribute to data-driven decision-making, and build solutions that create measurable business value. Every project I build is another step toward becoming a better analyst and solving real-world problems through data.
