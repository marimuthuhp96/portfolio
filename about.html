About Me

The Mindset

"I don't start with dashboards. I start with questions."

Every dataset holds more than numbers — it holds opportunities to discover patterns, solve problems, and make better decisions. My passion for data analytics began with a curiosity to understand why things happen, not just what happened. That curiosity led me to complete my Master of Science in Data Analytics, where I developed both the technical skills and analytical mindset to work with real-world data.

Throughout my journey, I have built end-to-end analytics projects using SQL, Python, Power BI, and Advanced Excel. From cleaning and transforming raw datasets to designing interactive dashboards and applying machine learning techniques, I enjoy converting complex information into clear, actionable insights that support business decisions.

As I explored the evolving world of analytics, I became interested in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. This inspired me to build projects using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and GraphRAG, combining traditional analytics with AI to create intelligent, insight-driven applications that go beyond static reports.

I believe the best analysts don't simply present data — they ask meaningful questions, uncover hidden insights, and help organizations make confident decisions. Every project in this portfolio reflects that belief and represents my commitment to continuous learning, innovation, and solving real-world problems through data.

Today, I am seeking an opportunity to begin my professional career as a Data Analyst, where I can contribute, grow alongside experienced professionals, and create measurable business value through data-driven solutions.
